# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-16

Filament 3: records of processing activities (ROPA, LGPD art. 37) for [laravel-lgpd-records](https://github.com/jeffersongoncalves/laravel-lgpd-records).

- Processing activities: purpose, legal basis, data subjects, data categories, retention, security measures
- Link activities to `lgpd.models`: their declared personal data joins the record
- Recipients (processor, shared controller, public authority) and international transfers with their art. 33 mechanism
- Export ROPA (JSON): controller, data protection officer and every activity
- English and Brazilian Portuguese
